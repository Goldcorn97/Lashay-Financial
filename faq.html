GOOD QUESTIONS BELONG HERE

A few questions.
Some helpful answers.

A few helpful answers before your Medicare, life insurance, or retirement conversation.

I’m helping a parent or loved one. Where do I start?+

You’re welcome to explore these resources together. Ask your loved one what matters most to them and make a list of questions. They should remain involved in decisions about their coverage. Any permission needed to discuss their personal information can be arranged with the agency.

What should I ask about Medicare?+

Start with your doctors, prescriptions, expected costs, and enrollment timing. Ask how Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage differ, and where prescription drug coverage or Medicare Supplement Insurance may fit. Options depend on your eligibility and location. Visit Medicare.gov for official information.

Where do I start with life insurance?+

Think about who depends on you and the expenses you would want to help cover. Ask about term and permanent coverage, premiums, exclusions, and any waiting periods. Final expense coverage can be part of this conversation. Eligibility and benefits depend on the policy.

What should I consider for retirement income?+

Start with your expected expenses, existing income sources, savings, and access to emergency funds. If you explore an annuity, ask about costs, surrender charges, withdrawal restrictions, risks, and income terms. Guarantees depend on the issuing insurer’s financial strength and claims-paying ability. Learn more at Investor.gov.

Can I discuss Medicare and life insurance together?+

Keep Medicare plan discussions separate from life insurance and annuity sales conversations. Let the agency know which topic you want to discuss so the appropriate appointment and any required permissions can be arranged.

Does this website provide a quote or enroll me?+

No. The guide creates a checklist for you to download. It does not submit your information, provide a personalized recommendation, or enroll you in coverage. Quotes, product availability, and eligibility must be confirmed with the agency and applicable insurer.
