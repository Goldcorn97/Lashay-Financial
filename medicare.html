YOUR HEALTH. YOUR QUESTIONS.

Let’s make Medicare
easier to understand.

Approaching Medicare or reviewing your options? Start with the coverage questions that matter to your everyday life.

01

Understand your options

Ask how Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, prescription drug coverage, and Medicare Supplement Insurance differ. They are not interchangeable, and the right questions depend on your situation.

02

Think about your everyday care

Make a list of the doctors, hospitals, prescriptions, and pharmacies you use. Verify coverage and network details directly before making a plan decision.

03

Look at the full picture

Compare premiums and out-of-pocket costs, coverage rules, and enrollment timing. A low premium alone does not tell you the total cost of coverage.

Before a Medicare appointment

A website checklist is not a Scope of Appointment or an enrollment application. Any required Medicare appointment permissions and timing must be handled separately. Life insurance and annuity sales conversations should be scheduled separately from Medicare Advantage or Part D marketing appointments.
