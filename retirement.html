YOUR FUTURE. YOUR TERMS.

For the retirement
you look forward to.

Whether you’re getting ready to retire or already enjoying life after work, begin with questions about income, everyday expenses, and keeping room for the unexpected.

01

Picture life after work

Think about essential expenses, time with family, travel, and the everyday life you want. A clear picture helps you ask more meaningful questions.

02

Review your income picture

Consider existing retirement income sources, savings, and future needs. Ask how changing expenses and access to emergency funds fit into the conversation.

03

Understand annuities

An annuity is an insurance contract that may provide income immediately or later. Ask about fees, surrender charges, withdrawal restrictions, risks, and the conditions attached to any guarantees.

Know the details before deciding

Annuities are long-term products, and features vary by contract. Guarantees depend on the issuing insurer’s financial strength and claims-paying ability. This page provides general education, not personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. Confirm available services and products with the agency.
